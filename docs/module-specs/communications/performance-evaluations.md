- Open evaluation cycles.
- Collect rubric scores and comments.
- Track improvement actions.

## Admin Surface
- Filters: chapter, evaluator, overdue review.
- Metrics: completed vs pending evaluations.
- Lens: users with repeated low scores or no follow-up.
