# Activity Risk

- Status: `Missing`
- Priority: `P2`
- Depends on: Programs, Workouts, Route Difficulty, Environmental Conditions

## Purpose
- Model sport- and activity-level risk so session templates and events can inherit operating guidance.

## Minimum Records
- activity_risks with sport, activity type, risk band, mitigations, approval requirements

## Core Workflows
- Assign risk profile to workouts and events.
- Drive staffing, equipment, and incident-prevention rules.
- Surface elevated-risk sessions in dashboards.

## Admin Surface
- Filters: sport, risk band.
- Metrics: upcoming high-risk sessions.
- Lens: sessions lacking risk classification.

## Implementation Note
- Use it to drive staffing and escalation rules, not just a passive label.
